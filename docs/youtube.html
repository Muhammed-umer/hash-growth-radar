YouTube collection

How the radar finds videos, checks them for new comments, reads those comments, and ranks the questions. Every number on this page is taken from the code: src/lib/config.ts, src/lib/youtube/, src/lib/pipeline/ and supabase/migrations/. Written 21 Sep 2026.

1 · The allowance

Google gives the YouTube Data API two separate daily pots. They reset at midnight Pacific time.

100 searchesa day. One search page is one search.
10,000 unitsa day for every other call. One page is one unit.
95 / 9,000where our jobs stop, to stay clear of Google's limit.

Every call is added to the quota_ledger table before it is made. When a pot reaches its stop line, the job ends cleanly and the next run carries on where it left off.

2 · The jobs

The database runs seven jobs on a timer (Supabase Cron). Times are UTC. Nobody presses anything.

3 · Finding videos

Every video found is saved once in the videos table, the watch list. Comments are always read from that table, never straight from a search. Four routes feed it.

The search limit: 50 per page, about 500 per search

One search call returns at most 50 videos. More come by asking for the next page, but YouTube stops after about 10 pages. So one search reaches about 10 × 50 = 500 videos, and each page costs one of the 100 daily searches.

  1. Sweep: older videos, once (every 2 hours until done) Each of the 6 topics is searched one calendar month at a time, newest month first, back to 1 January 2026, sorted once by views and once by date. If a month still fills all 10 pages, the code assumes it hit the 500 limit, splits that window in half, and searches each half, later half first, down to 1-day windows. It saves its page after every call, so a run can stop anywhere. It uses at most 64 searches a day, leaving the rest for Discover.
  2. Discover: new videos (every 6 hours) Each topic is searched for videos uploaded since its last search (minus 1 hour of overlap), newest first, up to 5 pages. If more than 5 pages are new, the rest of that window is handed to the Sweep. Once a day per topic, one extra search sorted by relevance catches popular videos.
  3. Channels: every upload of every relevant creator (daily) A channel that made one on-topic video is followed. Its uploads list gives every video it publishes, 50 per unit, with no search needed. Each day the newest page of each followed channel is read (up to 600 channels, up to 3 pages if all were new). A newly followed channel also has its older uploads read once, up to 40 pages, at most 2,000 units a run.
  4. Coverage: the check (weekly) 20 random followed channels have their full upload list compared with the watch list. Anything missing is added.

4 · Checking for new comments

Checking and reading are two different steps. Checking is cheap; reading costs more, so it only happens when needed.

  1. Check the count (every 2 hours, only videos that are due) The Collect job asks YouTube for the current comment count of up to 1,000 due videos, 50 per call, 1 unit per call. It compares each with the count stored last time.
  2. Decide whether to read A video's comments are read when any of these is true: it has never been read; a previous read stopped half-way; its comment count changed; it was uploaded in the last 7 days; it has not been read for 7 days. Live streams are skipped until they end. Shorts are read at most once a week.

Not every video is checked every 2 hours

Each video carries its own next-check time, set by how active it is:

Video stateNext count check
Comments just changed, or uploaded in the last 7 days2 hours
No change for less than 60 days1 day
No change for 60 days or more7 days
No change for 180 days and over 180 days oldRetired, no more checks

So an old video that suddenly gets a new question is caught at its next check: within 2 hours if it is active, a day if it is quiet, a week if it has been quiet for two months.

5 · Reading comments

  • Comments are fetched newest first, 100 per page, with the first replies under each.
  • The read stops at the last comment already saved (the video's cursor). So each read collects only what is new.
  • On a video's first read it goes back to 1 January 2026. Nothing older is ever kept.
  • The creator's own comments are dropped. The commenter's channel id is compared with the video's channel in memory and then thrown away. No username is stored anywhere.
  • What is kept: the comment text, when it was posted, its likes, whether it is a reply, the video and channel it came from, and a link that opens YouTube on that comment.
  • The cursor moves only after the comments are saved. If a read hits its page limit, it saves its place and continues from there next run. Nothing is skipped.

7 · Filter, AI and score

Word check (free, no AI)

A comment is dropped if it is under 15 characters, posted before 1 January 2026, names no medicine, condition, app or food, does not look like a question, or looks like spam. Most comments stop here.

AI (Gemini)

Each surviving comment gets a fixed form: what kind of question it is, which conditions and medicines it names, how well Hash answers it (the fit, 0 to 100), how urgent it is, whether the person is suitable to approach, and a one-line summary. Comments asking about a dose, starting or stopping a medicine, a side effect, a diagnosis or a lab result, describing an emergency, or about eating disorders, mental health, pregnancy, breastfeeding or a child under 18 are marked not suitable and never reach the list.

Score

StepPoints

Medicine + food question / asking for an app / complaint about an app / nutrition question+15 / +10 / +5 / 0

Each full day since the comment was posted, for the first 7 days−2 per day, at most −14
The age penalty follows the comment, not the video. A comment posted yesterday under a 2024 video loses 2 points. A comment posted weeks ago under any video loses the full 14 as soon as it is scored. Scores are refreshed every hour, so a new comment's number drops by 2 each time it passes another full day, until day 7.

The Shortlist shows medicine, app and complaint questions with a score of 70 or more, posted in the last 30 days, 25 a page. The YouTube page lists everyone, 25 a page, with filters.

9 · Clean-up

  • Stored comments are deleted 30 days after YouTube last returned them, as YouTube's developer policy requires.
  • Videos that were removed or made private are deleted after 30 days. Retired videos and videos with comments turned off lose their title and counts and keep only their id and status.
  • A channel with no on-topic upload for 120 days stops being followed, and is deleted 30 days later.
  • Finished AI tasks are deleted after 30 days, and run logs after 60 days.
